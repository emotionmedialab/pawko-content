Let's teach your dog to tell you a secret! Grab treats and a quiet room. Kneel down to your dog's level. Hold your fingers right next to your ear, and wait for a sniff. When their nose touches your fingers, reward. Next, take your fingers away. Reward when your dog moves toward your ear. Now say "tell a secret" before you lean in. Keep your voice soft. If your dog gets bouncy and excited, the game got too loud. Stay quiet and calm, and take a short break if you need one. Say yes, or click, then reward!
