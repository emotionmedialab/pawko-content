Let's teach your dog to wait, even when the treat is right there! Grab treats and a quiet space. Drop a treat on the floor and cover it with your hand. When your dog backs away, reward from your other hand. Next, wait a few seconds longer each time, then say "okay" to release. Now add one temptation, like a toy on the floor. Then use it every day, before meals and before greeting people. If your dog dives in, you changed too much at once. Change only one thing, and make it easier. Say yes, or click, then reward!
