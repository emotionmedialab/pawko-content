Let's teach your dog to stay, even when you walk away! Grab treats and some space. Ask for a sit and say "stay". Take one step back, return, and reward. Then two steps, then five. Always come back to your dog before the release. Next, walk a small circle around them. Feed treats while your dog is still sitting. Don't call them to you, or the stay ends. Then say "okay" and toss a treat. If your dog gets up, quietly reset. It was just too hard for now. Every time you return, say yes, or click, then reward!
