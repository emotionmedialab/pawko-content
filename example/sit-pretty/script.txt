Let's teach your dog to sit pretty! Grab treats and a non-slip floor. Skip this one for growing puppies, and for dogs with sore hips, backs or joints. Ask for a sit. Hold a treat just above the nose and lift it slowly. Reward any lift of the front paws. Then ask for a little longer with the paws up. Keep sessions short. Say "sit pretty" as your dog starts to rise. If your dog jumps or stands up, you lifted too high or too fast. Keep the treat close to the nose. Say yes, or click, then reward!
