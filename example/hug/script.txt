Let's teach your dog to give a hug! Only try this with a dog who loves close contact. Sit on the floor or a low chair, with your dog in front of you. Hold a treat above your shoulder, and guide your dog's front paws onto you. Reward. Next, reward when they rest their head on your shoulder. Keep it gentle. Then say "hug" as the paws go up. Keep nails trimmed. If your dog leans away or seems unsure, they may not enjoy hugs. Never hold them there. Keep it short and optional. Say yes, or click, then reward!
