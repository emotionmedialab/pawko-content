Let's teach your dog to rest in the crate for longer, the healthy way! Grab a chew toy, and plan some exercise. Leave your dog in the crate for one hour with the toy. Come back calmly. Between crate sessions, go for a walk and play. Keep a steady routine, because predictable days help dogs relax. The big mistake is crating too long. Puppies, about one hour per month of age. Adults, four to six hours at most. Need longer? Ask a friend or dog walker to help. Back to a calm dog? Say yes, or click, then reward!
