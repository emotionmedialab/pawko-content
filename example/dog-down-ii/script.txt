Let's teach your dog a rock-solid down! Grab some treats and a non-slip floor. Lure first: hold a treat at the nose and slowly lower it straight to the floor. Now fade the lure. Make the same movement with an empty hand, and reward from your other hand. Next, add the cue. Say "down" once, then give the hand signal. Reward the moment the elbows touch the floor. Practice from a stand, not just a sit. If your dog pops back up, you treated too late or too high. Treat sooner, and closer to the floor. Never push them down. Deliver treats while your dog stays down. Say yes, or click, then reward!
