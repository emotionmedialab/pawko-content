Let's teach your dog to give the right paw! Grab treats and a quiet room. Hold a treat in a closed fist on your dog's right side. Wait. The moment the right paw lifts, reward. Next, offer an open palm to the right. Reward as soon as the paw touches it. Now say "right paw" before you offer your hand. Once both paws are solid, ask for left and right in a random order. If your dog offers the wrong paw, they are guessing. Don't reward it. Reset, and reward only the correct paw. Say yes, or click, then reward!
