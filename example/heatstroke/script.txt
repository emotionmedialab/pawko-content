If your dog got heatstroke right now, would you know what to do? Save this, because when it happens, there's no time to look it up. It doesn't take an extreme day. A normal walk on a hot afternoon is enough. Watch for heavy panting, drooling, collapsing, or refusing to walk. If you see it, act fast. One, stop. The walk is over. Two, shade. Get them out of the sun and off the hot ground. Three, water. Pour cool tap water all over their body, not just the paws. No ice, and no wet towels on top. They trap the heat. Four, air. A fan or the AC. Five, call the vet while you keep cooling, and go even if they seem fine. Send this to every dog owner you know.
