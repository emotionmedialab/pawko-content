Let's teach your dog to hold a down, even when things get exciting! Grab treats and a mat. Ask for a down and wait three seconds before you mark. Build slowly to ten seconds, then twenty. Next, step to the side, turn around, and walk a small circle. Reward your dog for staying down. Now add a small distraction, like a quiet clap. End every down with "okay", so your dog stays until you say. If your dog gets up, you asked for too much. Shorten the time and move closer. Say yes, or click, then reward!
