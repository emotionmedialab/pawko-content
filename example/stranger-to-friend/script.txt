Let's teach your dog that visitors mean good things! Grab a helper and your best treats. Start far enough away that your dog notices the visitor but stays calm. The moment the visitor appears, feed treat after treat. When they leave, the treats stop. Slowly move closer as your dog relaxes. Ask the visitor to ignore your dog until your dog is calm. Let your dog choose to say hello when ready. Never force a greeting. If your dog barks or backs away, you are moving too fast. Add distance and slow down. Say yes, or click, then reward!
