Let's teach your dog to stay calm in the crate while you leave the room! Grab a chew toy and treats. Close the crate door, then step out of sight for thirty seconds. Come back calmly. Work up to one, then two, then five minutes. Always return before your dog gets upset. Mix short and long absences, so they can't predict the length. If you make a big fuss when you return, coming back becomes exciting. Keep it calm and quiet. When you come back to a calm dog, say yes, or click, then reward!
