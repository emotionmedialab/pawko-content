Let's teach your dog to go to place, even when the doorbell rings! Grab a mat, treats, and a helper. Stand a few steps away, say "place", and point to the mat. Reward when your dog goes there. Next, have your helper ring the bell. Reward your dog for going to the mat and staying. Then slowly build the time to a minute or more. Always release with "okay", so your dog knows it is over. If your dog leaves the mat, don't scold. Quietly reset, and make it a little easier. Say yes, or click, then reward!
