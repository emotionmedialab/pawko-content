Let's teach your dog to relax in the crate with the door closed! Grab a stuffed chew toy and treats. Give your dog the toy in the crate, close the door, and sit right next to it. Reward calm behavior. Start with ten seconds, then one minute, then five. Do several short sessions a day, and keep it low-key. If your dog whines, don't open the door yet. Opening now teaches that whining works. Wait for a short pause of quiet, then open. When they settle quietly, say yes, or click, then reward!
