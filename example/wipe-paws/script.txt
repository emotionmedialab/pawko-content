Let's teach your dog to wipe their paws! Grab treats and a small doormat. Put the mat on the floor near the door. Reward any paw that touches it. Next, wait for a little scrape. The moment a paw drags across the mat, reward. Reward every scrape, and soon you will see more of them. Now say "wipe paws" as your dog steps on. If you keep rewarding standing, you will only get standing. Wait for the scrape, then reward fast. Keep a towel handy for muddy days. Say yes, or click, then reward!
