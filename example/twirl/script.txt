Let's teach your dog to twirl, the other way from spin! Grab treats and a non-slip floor. Hold a treat at the nose. Move it in a wide circle at nose height, the opposite way from spin. When your dog finishes the full turn, reward. After a few reps, make the same circle with an empty hand, and reward from your other hand. Now say "twirl" just before the motion. Then mix it with spin. If your dog turns the wrong way, your two cues look too alike. Use a different word and hand. Say yes, or click, then reward!
