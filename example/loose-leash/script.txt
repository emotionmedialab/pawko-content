Let's teach your dog that a loose leash makes the walk go! Put on a comfy harness and leash, and start in a quiet hallway. Keep treats at your hip. Every few steps, when your dog is beside you with a loose leash, reward at your leg. The moment the leash gets tight, stand still. Wait for your dog to look back or step toward you. As soon as the leash goes slack, reward, and walk on. Never yank the leash back. Just stop, and let slack restart the walk. Say yes, or click, then reward!
