Let's teach your dog a rock-steady stay! Grab treats and a helper. Build up to thirty, then sixty seconds, rewarding often at first. Next, step out of sight for one second, return, and reward. Stretch that time slowly. Now have your helper walk by, bounce a ball, or ring the doorbell. Reward big when your dog holds it. Then try it with your back turned, or sitting in a chair. Release with "okay", then play. If your dog breaks, you added too much at once. Go back a step. Say yes, or click, then reward!
