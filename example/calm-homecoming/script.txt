Don't greet your dog when you get home — do it a different way. Your dog goes crazy when you walk in. Jumping, spinning, panting. We're told that's pure love. But when you make a fuss over a dog that's already this wound up, you're not rewarding love. You're rewarding stress. So try the five-minute rule. When you get home, ignore him completely. Don't look, don't touch, don't talk. I know it's hard. Take off your shoes. Put away your keys. Wait until he calms down, four paws on the floor. Then call him gently and give him a calm pet. Now your arrival isn't an emergency. It's just normal. And a calm dog is a happy dog.
