Let's teach your dog to follow you, even in busy places! Clip on a long line in a safe, open area. Walk away, then call your dog. Reward the moment they catch up. Next, add gentle activity, like a quiet park path, or a dog far away. Swap some treats for a quick game of tug. Staying with you becomes the best game around. Ignored a big distraction? Give a jackpot of several treats. If your dog struggles, you moved up too fast. Go back a level for a few reps. Say yes, or click, then reward!
