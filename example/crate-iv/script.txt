Let's teach your dog to stay relaxed while you leave home! Grab a chew toy, and a pet camera if you have one. Settle your dog in the crate. Step out the front door for one or two minutes, then come back. Slowly build to five, ten, twenty, then thirty minutes. Keep your goodbyes and hellos boring. Watch the camera while you are gone. If your dog panics, you went too long. Cut the time back, and build up again more slowly. When you come back to a calm dog, say yes, or click, then reward!
