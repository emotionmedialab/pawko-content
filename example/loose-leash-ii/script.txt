Let's teach your dog to walk nicely anywhere! Grab a leash and treats, and try a new spot. Walk in curves, make sharp turns, and stop now and then. Reward your dog for staying near you. After a good stretch, say "go sniff" and let them explore on a loose leash. Then call them back and keep walking. Next, pass interesting smells or a few people. Reward each time they choose you. If you cut treats too fast, the pulling comes back. Fade slowly, and use sniff breaks as rewards. Say yes, or click, then reward!
