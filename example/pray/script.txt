Let's teach your dog to pray! Grab treats and a low stool, or use your arm. Set the stool right in front of your dog. Once both front paws are up on it, hold a treat low, just under the stool. As your dog lowers their head between their paws, reward. Next, wait a few seconds before you reward, but only a few. Say "pray" as the head goes down. If your dog hops off, you held the pose too long. A few seconds is plenty, so reward sooner. Say yes, or click, then reward!
