Let's teach your dog to crawl! Grab treats and a soft, non-slip surface. Ask your dog to lie down. Hold a treat on the floor, just in front of their nose. Slide it slowly forward. Reward the tiniest scoot while they stay down. Then lure a little farther each time. Say "crawl" as your dog starts moving. If your dog stands up, the treat moved too fast or too high. Keep it on the floor and go slower. Only reward when elbows and hips stay down. Stop if your dog seems uncomfortable. Say yes, or click, then reward!
