Let's teach your dog to stick with you through every turn! Clip on the leash and head somewhere quiet outside. Walk a slow figure eight, or make a U-turn. When your dog catches up beside your leg, reward right there. Now change your speed. Walk fast, then slow, then stop. Reward your dog for matching you. Next, practice near a bush or a quiet toy. If your dog loses track of you, you added surprises too soon. Keep your turns predictable at first. Then reward every second or third time. Say yes, or click, then reward!
