## Prompt style that works (A/B tested: natural words beat camera specs)
"A still from a casual phone video filmed at home, phone held upright and perfectly level at chest-to-eye height, straight verticals, centered framing, no tilt. Plain home hallway: light grey walls, white doors with silver handles both sides, white baseboards, oak floor, beige runner rug, plain dome ceiling lights with a faint warm cast, cooler daylight from a far-end window slightly blown out. Normal everyday lighting, a bit dim, soft unremarkable shadows, a little grain in darker areas, everything in focus like a phone camera, slight motion blur on fast movement, typical phone sharpening. Nothing looks lit or styled. No text, no logo."
- Always state real-world scale vs the doors (standing head ≈ 85% of door height; kneeling head ≈ half).
- Avoid: "cinematic", camera spec lists (little effect), tilted framing, bright/even studio light.
- End frames: use the start frame as the first reference ("same video, last moment") so the room matches.
